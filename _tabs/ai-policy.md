## Use of Generative AI in My Blog

In the interest of transparency, I thought it would be appropriate to disclose exactly how I use AI in the production of my content. I sincerely believe that AI has a place in almost any workflow and certainly does in mine, but some comments from friends and colleagues have revealed that there are those who believe my blog posts are mostly AI-generated as opposed to hand-written.

I **do** make use of AI tools in the production of my blog, but that use is limited to three functions:

1. **Site maintenance and Jekyll changes.** To avoid spending too much time fussing with the Jekyll theme and templates, I let an AI assistant handle things like style changes and restructuring.
2. **Proofreading and fact checking.** Before I publish a post, I run an AI assistant that checks my spelling, grammar, and punctuation, as well as double-checking my facts.
3. **SEO and richness.** The same AI assistant also generates metadata, tags, and categories, and adds many of the links for SEO purposes. It also cleans up some of the fancier formatting, adds detailed accessibility captions to images, and fixes media embeds for me.

The purpose of these functions is to deliver the best possible experience for browsing my blog and to minimize the amount of tedious editing so I can focus on writing.

## What I Will Never Use AI For

The ideas presented in this blog are mine and do not originate from generative AI. I don't need AI to express my bizarre ideas; I can handle that myself. I will also not use AI to steal and rewrite someone else's writing and publish it as my own.

## My Guarantee

Any post on this blog that uses AI beyond the policy outlined above will include a disclaimer explaining what and why.
